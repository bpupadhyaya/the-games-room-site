Filament

Light a single closed loop through a grid of pegs. Each number says how many sides of its cell the wire uses.

PuzzleSoloLessons5x5 to 10x10Daily

Coming soon to theApp StoreComing soon toGoogle Play

The rules in a minute

  1. Draw one closed loop through the pegs, moving along the grid lines.
  2. A number in a cell says how many of its four sides the loop uses.
  3. Cells without a number are free. The loop never crosses or branches.
  4. When the loop closes and every number is met, the lamp lights.

In the app

  • Fresh puzzles generated for every game, each with exactly one solution
  • Easy, Medium and Hard, from 5 by 5 up to 10 by 10
  • Hints that explain the deduction, not just the answer
  • A pen for marking strands that are not part of the loop
  • Four short lessons: reading numbers, zeros and threes, corners and pegs, one loop only
  • A daily puzzle with the same grid for everyone, and a lamp that lights when you finish

Habits of good players

  • Start with the zeros and threes: they place strands straight away.
  • A corner peg has only two strands, so a loop that touches it takes both.
  • Close a loop too early and the numbers left outside cannot be met.
