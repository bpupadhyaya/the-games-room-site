Official app support page

When you write to us

  • Say which table you were at, Four in a Row or Hearts.
  • Tell us your device and its system version.
  • Describe what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.
  • Please do not send passwords or payment details. We never need them.

Quick answers

The app is not in the stores yet. The Games Room is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Until then the two web previews on this site are the way to try it.

A preview move looks wrong. The previews follow the standard rules, written on each table page. If you find a mistake, write to us with the moves you played.

Is anything stored? The previews keep nothing. Close the tab and the game is gone.
