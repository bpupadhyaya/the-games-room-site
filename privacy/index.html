Privacy

No account. Your results stay on your device. A few ads, with your consent.

The short version

A game needs only a table and a patch of light, so that is all the app asks for: your attention and a few spare minutes.

The app

Accounts
None. There is no sign-in and no profile.
Advertising
The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner in the room and an occasional ad when you leave a table, never during a game. Where the law requires it, Google shows a consent form first, and Settings has a Privacy options entry to change your choice later. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier and approximate location to choose and measure ads. The optional one-time Remove ads purchase turns every ad off, and watching one short video gives an ad-free hour.
Your results
Results, streaks and settings stay on your device.
Network
The games work fully offline. The only network traffic is the ad requests, and the store purchase if you choose Remove ads.

This website

The previews run entirely in your browser. Moves are not transmitted and nothing is stored when you close the page. The site sets no cookies of its own.

What that means for you

You can open the app, pick a seat and play without telling us who you are. We do not know which games you play, how well you do, or how long you stay; the only data that leaves your device is what the ad service needs to show and measure ads, and you choose that in the consent form. The only thing the store will ask of you is the usual single purchase, if you choose to remove the ads.

The formal policy

The complete policy lives with our other apps. Questions go to privacy@equalinformation.com.
