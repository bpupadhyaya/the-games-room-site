The Games Room / Privacy

Privacy

The Games Room collects no data, shows no ads and asks for no account. It works completely offline.

In the app

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile.
  • No advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • Your results, streaks and settings stay on your device.
  • Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else while you play.

On this website

The two web previews run entirely inside your browser. Your moves are not sent anywhere and nothing is saved when you close the page. This site sets no cookies of its own.
