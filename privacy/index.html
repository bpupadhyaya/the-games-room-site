Privacy

Nothing is collected. Nothing is shown. Nothing needs an account.

The short version

A game needs only a table and a patch of light, so that is all the app asks for: your attention and a few spare minutes.

The app

Accounts
None. There is no sign-in and no profile.
Advertising
None, and no tracking of any kind.
Your results
Results, streaks and settings stay on your device.
Network
It works fully offline and sends nothing while you play.

This website

The previews run entirely in your browser. Moves are not transmitted and nothing is stored when you close the page. The site sets no cookies of its own.

What that means for you

You can open the app, pick a seat and play without telling anyone who you are. We do not know which games you play, how well you do, or how long you stay. The only thing the future store listings will ask of you is the usual single purchase, if you choose to unlock the extras.

The formal policy

The complete policy lives with our other apps. Questions go to privacy@equalinformation.com.
