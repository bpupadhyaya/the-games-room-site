Questions

Good to know

Is The Games Room free?

Yes. Every game is free to play at its Easy and Medium levels, with hints and the first lesson. One optional, one-time unlock adds the Hard levels, every lesson, the Cabinet and extras. There is no subscription.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The Games Room works completely offline, on a plane or in a cabin.

Are there ads? Do you collect my data?

There are no ads, and the app collects no data and asks for no account. Your results, streaks and settings stay on your device.

When can I get it?

The Games Room is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. This site will link to the stores as soon as the app is available.

Which devices?

iPhone and iPad, and Android phones and tablets, in portrait and landscape.

Is the web preview the full game?

No. A preview on this site is a small demo of a game against the computer, at Easy and Medium. The app adds the Hard levels, guided lessons, daily games, the Cabinet, sound and richer animation.

Can I play against friends?

Several games have pass and play for two people on one device; each game page says what it offers.

How do I get help?

Write to support@equalinformation.com or open the support page. For privacy questions write to privacy@equalinformation.com.
