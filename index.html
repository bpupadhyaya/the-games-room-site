Questions

Good to know

Is The Games Room free?

Yes. Both tables are free to play against Easy and Medium opponents, with pass and play, hints and the first lesson at each table. One optional, one-time unlock adds the Hard opponents, every lesson, the Cabinet and extra disc sets. There is no subscription.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The Games Room works completely offline, on a plane or in a cabin.

Are there ads? Do you collect my data?

There are no ads, and the app collects no data and asks for no account. Your results, streaks and settings stay on your device.

When can I get it?

The Games Room is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. This site will link to the stores as soon as the app is available.

Which devices?

iPhone and iPad, and Android phones and tablets, in portrait and landscape.

Is the web preview the full game?

No. The preview on this site is a small demo of each table against the computer, at Easy and Medium. The app adds the Hard opponents, guided lessons, Tonight's Lamp, the Cabinet, extra disc sets, sound and richer animation.

Can I play against friends?

Four in a Row has pass and play for two people on one device. Hearts is you and three computer opponents. More tables may come to the room later.

How do I get help?

Write to support@equalinformation.com or open the support page. For privacy questions write to privacy@equalinformation.com.
