Hearts

The rules in a minute

  1. Everyone gets 13 cards. Before play, pass three to a neighbour: left, right, across, then hold with no pass.
  2. The 2 of clubs starts the first trick. Follow suit when you can.
  3. Highest card of the led suit wins the trick and leads next.
  4. No hearts and no Queen of spades on the first trick, unless you hold nothing else.
  5. Hearts may not lead until one has been played.
  6. Each heart costs 1 point and the Queen of spades costs 13. Fewer is better.
  7. Take every heart and the Queen to shoot the moon: you score 0 and the others take 26.
  8. The match ends when someone reaches 100; the lowest score wins.

At the table

Touch or mouse
Tap a card to play it. Dimmed cards are not legal.
Keyboard
Tab into your hand, arrow keys to move, Enter or Space to play.
Passing
Pick three cards, then Pass cards. Hint suggests three.
Hint and Undo
Hint explains a good play. Undo takes back your last card.

Habits of good players

  • Pass the Queen of spades and the high spades unless you hold plenty of small ones to guard them.
  • Slip under the winning card whenever you can.
  • Shedding a big heart or the Queen on a trick you cannot win is the heart of the game.
