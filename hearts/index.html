Hearts

Four players, one match to 100. Play the free preview right here against three computer opponents.

A demo of the table. Hard opponents, lessons and the Cabinet are in the app.

How to play

  • Each hand, everyone starts with 13 cards. Pass three cards left, right or across, then once with no passing; the pattern repeats.
  • Whoever holds the 2 of clubs leads it. Follow suit if you can.
  • The highest card of the led suit takes the trick and leads the next.
  • No hearts or Queen of spades on the first trick, unless you hold nothing else.
  • Hearts cannot lead until a heart has been played ("broken").
  • Each heart costs 1 point; the Queen of spades costs 13. Low score is good.
  • Take all 13 hearts and the Queen and you shoot the moon: you score 0 and the others take 26 each.
  • The match ends when someone reaches 100. The lowest score wins.

Controls and tips

  • Touch or mouse: tap a card to play it. Dimmed cards are not legal right now.
  • Keyboard: Tab into your hand, left and right arrows to move, Enter or Space to play.
  • Passing: select three cards, then press Pass cards. Hint suggests a sensible three.
  • Undo takes back your last card; Hint shows a good play and why.

Ideas

  • Pass the Queen of spades and high spades unless you have plenty of low ones to protect them.
  • Duck under the winning card whenever you can.
  • Shedding a high heart or the Queen on a trick you cannot win is the whole game.
