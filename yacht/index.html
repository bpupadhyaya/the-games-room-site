Yacht

Throw five glass dice, keep the ones you like and write the best line on an ink score card.

DiceSoloTwo playersLessonsDaily

The rules in a minute

  1. Throw all five dice. You may keep any and re-roll the rest, twice.
  2. After your last roll, write the result on one empty line of the card.
  3. Upper lines (Aces to Sixes) add up matching dice; reach 63 there for a 35 point bonus.
  4. Lower lines pay for Choice, Four of a Kind, Full House, straights and Yacht, five of a kind.
  5. When all twelve lines are filled, the card is complete and the total is your score.

In the app

  • Solo score chase, a game against the computer, or two players
  • Twelve categories and an upper-section bonus, with a preview of each line before you write it
  • A coach hint that weighs the odds and explains its choice
  • Hold, re-roll and undo before you commit
  • Short lessons on holding, choosing a line, the bonus and risk
  • A daily game with the same dice for everyone

Habits of good players

  • Early on, chase the upper lines that make the 63 bonus reachable.
  • A line you cannot fill is not wasted if you spend it on a poor roll.
  • Keep pairs for a Full House or Four of a Kind; keep runs for straights.
