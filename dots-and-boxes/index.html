Dots and Boxes

Vermilion and indigo silk threads, enamel pegs on linen, and a wax seal for every box you close.

StrategyTwo playersLessons3 levelsDaily

The rules in a minute

  1. Players take turns stretching one thread between two neighbouring pegs.
  2. Complete the fourth side of a box and a wax seal marks it as yours.
  3. Closing a box earns another turn, so keep going while you can.
  4. When every thread is placed, the player with more boxes wins.

In the app

  • Play the house at three levels, or pass and play
  • Cloths in three sizes: 4 by 4, 5 by 5 and 6 by 6 boxes
  • A needle hint that shows a safe thread and tells you why
  • Four short lessons: boxes, safe moves, chains and the double-cross
  • A daily endgame puzzle

Habits of good players

  • Avoid giving the third side of a box: it hands your rival a seal.
  • Near the end the board splits into chains. Count them before you commit.
  • Sometimes it pays to decline the last two boxes of a chain to keep control.
