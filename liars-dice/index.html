Liar's Dice

Everyone shakes dice under a cup. Bid how many of a face are on the table, and call the bluff when you do not believe it.

The rules in a minute

  1. Each player shakes their dice under a cup and looks at their own.
  2. In turn, raise the bid: more dice, or the same number of a higher face.
  3. Instead of raising you may call. Every cup is lifted and the claim is counted.
  4. If the table holds at least as many as claimed, the caller loses a die. If not, the bidder does.
  5. Lose all your dice and you are out. The last cup standing wins.

In the app

  • You against three rivals: Ash is careful, Ember bluffs and Cinder leaps at the count
  • Ones can be wild, or play with plain dice
  • A full game of five dice each, or a quick game of three
  • A hint that gives the odds and says why
  • Short lessons on odds, bidding, bluffing and wild ones
  • A daily round with the same dice for everyone

Habits of good players

  • Start from what is in your own cup: it is the only evidence you have.
  • Opening a little low gives nothing away.
  • Learn how each rival bids before you decide whom to doubt.
