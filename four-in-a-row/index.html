How to play

  • You play red; the house plays ivory. Take turns dropping one disc into a column.
  • Discs fall to the lowest free slot in the column.
  • The first to connect four discs in a straight line wins: across, up and down, or diagonally.
  • If the board fills with no four, the game is a draw.

Controls

  • Touch or mouse: tap or click a column.
  • Keyboard: press 1 to 7, or Tab to a column, use the left and right arrows, then Enter.
  • Hint suggests a column. Undo takes back your last move and the house reply.

A few ideas

  • The middle column is part of the most winning lines. Start there.
  • Look for two threats at once: a double threat cannot be blocked.
  • Watch the cell above a threat. Sometimes you must not play under it.
  • On Medium the house looks several moves ahead. Plan a trap, not a line.
