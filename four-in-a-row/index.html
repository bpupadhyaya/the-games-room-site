The rules in a minute

  1. You are red, the house is ivory. Players alternate, one disc per turn.
  2. A disc falls to the lowest empty slot of the column you choose.
  3. Line up four of your colour, sideways, upright or on a diagonal, and you win.
  4. No four and no room left? The game is drawn.

At the table

Touch or mouse
Tap or click any column.
Keyboard
Press 1 to 7, or Tab to a column, move with the arrow keys and press Enter.
Hint
Points at a good column.
Undo
Takes back your last disc and the house reply.

Habits of good players

  • Own the middle column early; it sits on the most lines.
  • Build two threats at once. A double threat cannot be blocked.
  • Mind the square above a threat: playing beneath it can hand over the win.
  • Medium looks several moves ahead, so set a trap rather than a straight line.
