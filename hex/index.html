Hex

A limestone slab, shallow hollows and teal and amber sea-glass stones under a hanging lamp. Link your two shores before your rival does.

StrategyTwo playersLessons3 levelsDaily

The rules in a minute

  1. Teal links the west and east shores; amber links north and south.
  2. Players take turns placing one stone on any empty hollow. Stones never move or leave.
  3. The first side to complete an unbroken chain between its two shores wins.
  4. A game never ends in a draw: when the board is full, exactly one side has a chain.
  5. With the swap rule on, the second player may take over the first stone instead of replying.

In the app

  • Play the house at three levels, or hand the device to a friend
  • An 11 by 11 board, or a quicker 9 by 9
  • A swap rule you can switch on, so the first move stays fair
  • A hint with the reason behind it, and take-backs
  • Four short lessons: connecting, blocking ladders, bridges and the swap
  • A daily position: win it in a few stones

Habits of good players

  • The centre is worth more than the edge: start near it.
  • Two stones with two shared empty hollows between them are linked, even if the rival cuts one.
  • Blocking from a distance usually beats blocking up close.
